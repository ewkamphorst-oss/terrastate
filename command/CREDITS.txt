Hidden Command (Sealed Orders, Veiled Legions, Hidden Command) for playterrastate.nl
Made by Wim Kamphorst with the help of AI tools.

Artwork created with Google Gemini.
  The standees, board tiles, frames, river, bridges, hedgerows, minefields, jamming terrain, fog, table and box lids
  are renders made by Wim Kamphorst with Google Gemini, cut out and resized for the game (art-pipeline.py).

Game rules, board layouts, units, icons, the drawn fallback art and code are original to this game.

Sound effects: Kenney.nl, CC0 (public domain), https://kenney.nl
  Packs Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Impact Sounds and Sci-fi Sounds, mixed and re-encoded to mono mp3,
  some shortened with a fade out. Files used (names from the playterrastate.nl sound set):
  Ironfront: inslag, punt, gevlucht, fout
  Romaterra: gevecht, inslag, gevlucht, punt, fout
  Terrastate: gevecht, inslag, schot, breuk, punt, fout

The soft wooden click of a piece being placed is synthesized in the browser (Web Audio), no recording.

Fonts: Archivo, IBM Plex Mono and Cinzel, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
