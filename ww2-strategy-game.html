WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Every tick is a decision

Ironfront stays a tick-based game. But between two ticks you prepare an attack, or you defend your own ground and your alliance.

1 · BATTLE ORDERS
Guess right, win the tick

A battle lasts three ticks. Each tick both sides give an order, and the side that guesses right gets +25% that tick. Hold the line is the safe choice: no edge, no risk.

2 · PREPARE THE GROUND
A race against the landing

Trenches are ready in 1 tick, a minefield in 2, a bunker in 3. Only where an enemy is coming, and they cost treasury. Start too late and they are not ready.

3 · FOG AND DECEPTION
Radio traffic, not certainty

An incoming army shows as radio traffic with a size estimate. Better radar narrows it, a successful scan shows the real army. A feint looks three times bigger on weaker radar and turns back before it lands.

4 · HOLD THE LINE
Help that is there in time

Allied help that is in place when the battle starts counts in full, later help counts half. The Front board shows your alliance's fronts, soonest first, and who is on the way.

THE PRIZE
Two of three Capital Sectors

Not one prize but three. Hold two at once with a GHQ Bunker for 12 ticks, alone or as one alliance. Or hold one the longest in the Last Battle.

FAIR AI
The same rules for everyone

The computer nations give orders, dig in and send feints by the same rules, pay the same costs and see no more than their radar shows.

THE ORDER CYCLE

Which order beats which

⛏ DIG IN

beats Assault, loses to Barrage

⚡ COUNTER-ATTACK

beats Barrage, loses to Flank

🎯 AMBUSH

beats Flank, loses to Assault

The attacker chooses Assault, Barrage or Flank. With radar or recon you see what the other side did last tick, never what they will do next.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is Ironfront free?

Yes. Ironfront is completely free: no payments, no ads, no account and no download. It is a non-commercial hobby project by Wim Kamphorst from the Netherlands.

Is it a historical WW2 game?

No. Ironfront is set in a 1940s style world at war, but the nations, leaders and flags are invented and generated by the game. It does not retell the history of the Second World War.

How do you win?

Hold 2 of the 3 Capital Sectors at once with a GHQ Bunker for 12 ticks, alone or as one alliance. If nobody manages that, the Last Battle decides: whoever holds a Capital Sector the longest wins the season.

What is different from Terrastate and Romaterra?

The season, alliances and fair AI are the same. Ironfront adds its own battle layer: battle orders every tick, field works on a threatened region, radio traffic with size estimates and feints, and allied help that counts in full only when it is there in time. And it has three prizes instead of one.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Ironfront runs in any modern browser on phone and desktop, with a big END TURN button under your thumb on phones. You can install it as an app and play offline.

Is there multiplayer?

Not yet. What you play now is Season 0: single-player against 300 AI nations. The live world opens when 100 commanders have joined the waitlist on the Terrastate Discord.

What games is Ironfront like?

Ironfront is a tick-based war game in the browser, in the tradition of Planetarion, with a 1940s theme. Players who like WW2 strategy in the browser, such as Call of War, or grand strategy such as Hearts of Iron, will recognise the fronts, the logistics and the alliances. The difference: you play against hundreds of AI nations, at your own pace.
