Free browser strategy games like Planetarion and OGame, without the waiting

If you enjoy persistent browser strategy games such as Planetarion or OGame, with their alliances, scouting, timed attacks and a world that keeps moving, Terrastate takes that style and makes it playable on your own terms. It is free, it runs in any browser and you do not need an account.

What feels familiar

  • An economy you build up: money, food, energy, steel, fuel and electronics.
  • Alliances that sign pacts, declare war, scout and strike together.
  • Scouting before you attack, and battles that follow unit counters and doctrines.
  • One prize that decides the season: the Nexus in Terrastate, Roma in Romaterra.

Who it is for

Terrastate suits you if you want a free browser strategy game with no download, grand strategy on a phone, or a Planetarion-style game without having to wait for other players. A season lasts 200, 400 or 840 turns, and you can stop and pick up again whenever you like.

Who it is not for

If you want to compete against real players right now, Terrastate is not there yet. What you play today is Season 0: single-player against AI nations, the training ground for a live world. That live world opens when 100 commanders have joined the waitlist on the Terrastate Discord. Players from Season 0 are marked as Founding Commanders.

Two eras, one ruleset

Terrastate is set in an alternative 2026 with 25 theaters from Europe to Oceania. Romaterra is the same game in the age of Rome, with legionaries, cataphracts, ballistae and war elephants. Choose one, or play both.

Questions

Is there a free browser game like Planetarion that you can play alone?

Terrastate is inspired by tick-based browser strategy games such as Planetarion. You lead one nation against 300 AI nations, with alliances, scouting and timed attacks, at your own pace.

Is Terrastate pay-to-win?

No. It is a free, non-commercial hobby project with no payments and no ads.

Where do I start?

Read the beginner's guide or just press play. New players get Field Training, five small tasks and a practice battle.
