How to play Terrastate

Terrastate is a free grand strategy game that runs in your browser. You lead one nation against 300 AI nations on a world map of 25 theaters, and the season is decided by one region: the Nexus. This guide takes you from your first click to your first win. No account and no download are needed.

Step 1: Start with Field Training

New players get Field Training: five small tasks that teach you by doing, followed by a war game that explains your first battle. It takes a few minutes and you can skip it at any time.

Step 2: Choose your season

You decide how long a game lasts and how hard it is.

  • Quick: 200 turns
  • Standard: 400 turns
  • Epic: 840 turns

Difficulty is Recruit, Veteran or Warlord. If you want to learn the rules in a guided way, the game also has six campaign scenarios plus a Veteran round.

Step 3: Build your economy

Your nation runs on money, food, energy, steel, fuel and electronics. The market price you read is the price you pay, and the AI nations pay exactly what you pay.

Step 4: Research, raise an army, pick a doctrine

Your units include infantry, battle tanks, strike fighters, drones, powered armor and Titan mechs. Battles resolve in phases (air, recon and ground), with unit counters and doctrines deciding who comes out ahead.

Step 5: Join or found an alliance

Alliances sign pacts, declare war, scout and strike together. Rival alliances have a war council that picks targets and acts together, but always under the same rules as you.

Step 6: Take the Nexus and hold it

To win, take the Nexus, build the Earth Command Center on it and hold it for 12 ticks. If nobody manages that, the Last Battle decides: the whole world marches on the Nexus and whoever holds it the longest wins.

Turn speed: play at your own pace

Terrastate is turn-based. End each turn yourself, or let the world run automatically at 1x, 5x or 20x speed. Nothing waits for other players, so you can put the game down whenever you like and your saved game stays in your own browser. It also works on a phone and, once installed from the browser, offline.

Tips for your first season

  • Do the Field Training first, even if you know similar games.
  • Pick a Quick season on Recruit to learn how battles and alliances work.
  • Once the basics feel natural, switch to auto-play at 5x or 20x and step in when something happens.
  • Watch your personal rival: every season an AI rival seeks you out, and it returns the next season.

Playing Romaterra

Romaterra is the same game set in the age of Rome. You gather denarii, grain, timber, iron, livestock and bronze, train legionaries, cataphracts, ballistae and war elephants, and fight to take Roma. To win, raise the Imperial Palace and hold it for 48 ticks, or hold it the longest in the Last Battle.

Questions new players ask

What should a new Terrastate player do first?

Start with Field Training. It teaches the basics through five small tasks and one practice battle.

How long is a Terrastate season?

Quick is 200 turns, Standard is 400 and Epic is 840. You can end turns yourself or let the game run at 1x, 5x or 20x.

Is there multiplayer?

Not yet. What you play now is Season 0: single-player against AI nations, the training ground for a live world. The live world opens when 100 commanders have joined the waitlist on the Terrastate Discord. Players from Season 0 are marked as Founding Commanders.

Does the computer cheat?

No. The AI nations pay exactly what you pay and only know what they have scouted.
