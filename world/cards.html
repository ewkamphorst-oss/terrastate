Every unit and commander in this world has a card. Play the game on this device and every unit you train or commander you hire unlocks its card here.

Printing: the cards come out at real card size (63 x 88 mm), nine per A4 page. Turn on "Background graphics" in your print settings. Locked cards print in full colour too. After the fronts come pages with card backs, mirrored so they line up when you print on both sides.
