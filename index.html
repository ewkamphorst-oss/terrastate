IN ACTION

Same fight, two eras

Real screens from version 1.0, taken in the last ticks of a season when the whole world marches on the prize.

Line held. Twenty-one times outgunned, and still the flag stays yours. Every battle report says in one line what happened, what you lost, what they lost, and what to do next.

HOW IT PLAYS

One turn, three moves

1
READ
Who lands, and when

Home shows what is in the air: incoming in red, help in green, your own strikes in blue. One next action on top.

2
ORDER
Build, recruit, scout, strike

Each order costs command points. Scout before you sail: a rival's strength is unknown until your scan lands.

3
END TURN
The world answers

Every nation moves at once. Battles resolve, markets shift, alliances call for help. Or let it run at 1x, 5x or 20x.

WHAT YOU GET

A world that answers back

NEIGHBOURS
Nations with a temperament

Some raid, some hold, some are rich and thin. They trade, ally, betray and remember a strike.

COMBAT
Battles in phases

Air, recon and ground, unit counters and doctrines. A smaller combined force beats a bigger blind one.

ALLIANCES
Friends who answer

Found a bloc, share intel, call for defence and plan joint strikes. An alliance win is shared.

ECONOMY
A market that tells the truth

The price you read is the price you pay. Buy into a hot market and it costs you.

CAMPAIGN
Six scenarios

Learn the game one clear goal at a time, from your first regions to a season on Warlord.

YOUR TEMPO
Turn-based, or let it run

Quick, Standard or Epic. Recruit, Veteran or Warlord. Save slots, and it works offline.

COMMUNITY

Tell us your season

Questions, tips, screenshots and bug reports live on the Discord. It is also where you tell us whether you want real online multiplayer.
