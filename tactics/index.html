How it works

You give all your units an order and end the turn. Then everyone moves at once, archers fire, lines clash and morale wavers. Hold the flags to score points.

On your phone

Open a game and choose 'Add to home screen' in your browser. It then starts like an app, even without internet. Your progress lives in the browser; a progress code takes it to another device.

With a friend

Duel on one device (taking turns) or at a distance with turn codes you send each other in a message. No server needed.
